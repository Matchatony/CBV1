What we do

We CNC-cut 1/8 in. aluminum and polycarbonate plates and 3D-print parts, made to drawings or files you supply. We don't design your robot for you — we cut and print what you send us, or what we agree on together if you ask us to model something from a sketch.

Quotes and pricing

A quote we give by email is an estimate based on what you've told us or sent us. It isn't final until we confirm it in writing after seeing the actual file. If your file turns out to be more expensive to cut than described — more material, tighter tolerances, extra setup — we'll tell you before we start, not after.

Ordering

You send us a DXF, STEP, STL, or a dimensioned sketch through the quote form or by email. We confirm price and turnaround before cutting anything. Payment method and timing are agreed between us by email — we don't take payment through this website.

We don't claim ownership of anything you send us. We may keep a copy of your files for our own records, or to reproduce the same part again if you ask.

Turnaround and delivery

1–3 days is our normal turnaround, not a guarantee — material availability, order volume, or a competition crunch can push it out, and we'll tell you if that's happening. Parts are picked up in San Diego or delivered locally as arranged; shipping outside that isn't something we currently offer.
