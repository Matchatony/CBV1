Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026

The short version: we only ask for what we need to quote and build your part, we don't sell it or use it for ads, and we don't run analytics or tracking scripts on this site. The rest of this page is the detail behind that.

Who we are

Crackbotics is an independent, unincorporated student-run business based in San Diego, California — not a registered company. This policy covers crackbotics.com and the quote form on it. Questions about it go to anthonyhuynh980@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect information in exactly one place: the "Get a quote" form. That's:

  • Your name and email — required, so we can reply.
  • Team number — optional, helps us route parts for events.
  • Part details — type, material, size, quantity, and the options you pick, so we can price and make it.
  • A design file — required. A DXF, STEP, STL or 3MF you upload so we can quote and cut or print the part. DXF files are measured in your own browser to price the job; the file itself only leaves your computer when you submit the form.
  • A delivery address — only if you choose local delivery instead of pickup. See the section on address lookups below.
  • Anything you write in the details field.

We don't have user accounts, we don't collect payment details on the site (payment is arranged by email once a quote is agreed), and we don't ask for anything beyond what's in that form.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your request and, if you go ahead, to make the part and get it to you. Submitting the form is you asking us to use your information for that. We don't use it to build a marketing list, and we don't message you about anything other than the request you sent.

Who it's shared with

The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form backend that emails us your submission — we don't run our own server. Formspree's handling of that data is covered by their own privacy policy. We don't share, sell, or rent your information to anyone else, and we don't run ads.

Delivery address lookups

If you pick local delivery and start typing an address into the quote form, your browser sends what you've typed so far to Nominatim (run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) as you type, to show matching address suggestions to pick from. That request goes straight from your browser to Nominatim, covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy — we never see it. Local delivery is a flat $30 regardless of distance, so nothing about the address affects the price; all we keep is the address you submit with the form.

It only happens if you choose local delivery and type in the address field. Pickup orders never trigger a lookup, and you can leave the address blank and sort delivery out with us by email instead.

Fonts and other third-party requests

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them, subject to Google's privacy policy. The "Check out our CAD release" link on the About page takes you to a public document on Onshape's site — that's an outbound link, not something embedded in this page, and visiting it is covered by Onshape's privacy policy, not ours.

We don't run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other analytics or advertising script on this site.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies, and we don't use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking. We don't store anything in your browser's local storage either.

The two third-party services above may behave differently under their own policies: Formspree may set a cookie on their own domain when you submit the form (only after you choose to submit it), and Google's font delivery is documented as not using cookies for the font request itself. Neither is something we control or read.

Because we don't use non-essential cookies of our own, this site doesn't show a cookie-consent banner. If that changes — for example, if we ever add analytics — we'll update this section and add a consent banner before we do.

How long we keep it

We keep quote-request information for as long as it takes to complete your order, plus a limited time after (up to about a year) in case you follow up or need a repeat part. If you'd like it deleted sooner, email us and we will.

Your choices

Email anthonyhuynh980@gmail.com any time to ask what we have on file for you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We don't sell personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of there. This site does not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because we don't track visitors in the first place.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13. If you're under 13, please have a parent, guardian, or team mentor submit the form on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
