Sponsored Team Program · 2026–2027 season

Become one of our ten sponsored teams

Each season, CrackBotics partners with a select ten FTC teams: teams that win on the field, lift up their communities off it, and share what they build so others can learn. If that's your team, we want to hear from you.

Applications close Sunday, October 25 at 11:59 pm Pacific.

What we look for

  • Competitive success Results on the field: awards, rankings, advancing.
  • Community involvement Outreach, events, helping other teams and new students.
  • Sharing your work Posting builds, CAD, code or how-tos so others can learn.
  • Spreading the word Helping other teams find CrackBotics, online and at events.
