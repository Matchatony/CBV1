Sponsored Team Program · 2026–2027 season

Get your team sponsored by CrackBotics

CrackBotics is excited to support FTC teams that are making an impact both on and off the field! We're looking to partner with approximately 10 FTC teams that demonstrate competitive success, community involvement, and a passion for sharing their work with others.

What we look for

  • Competitive success Results on the field: awards, rankings, advancing.
  • Community involvement Outreach, events, helping other teams and new students.
  • Sharing your work Posting builds, CAD, code or how-tos so others can learn.

Selected teams hear from us by email with the program details.
