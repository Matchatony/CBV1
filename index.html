Why us

  • Cheaper. We heavily undercut SendCutSend and goBILDA on the same parts.
  • Faster. We're local. Pickup or delivery in 1–3 days, with no shipping wait.
  • No minimums. One bracket or fifty standoffs — both are fine.
  • Built by FTC students. We're on teams ourselves, so you don't have to explain the constraints.

Who we are

Three students from Runtime Terror (FTC #22105) in San Diego and All Systems Go (FTC #21239) in Kentucky. We kept paying three times what a simple bracket should cost, and we already had the CAD skills and the machine time — so we started making parts for other teams at the price we'd want to pay.

How to order

Send us a DXF, a STEP file, or a dimensioned sketch with the quantity and thickness. We quote same day, machine in 1–3 days, and you pick up at the shop or we deliver locally. No account, no quote portal.
