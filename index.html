Why order from us

We're not trying to be a supplier. We're trying to be the fastest, cheapest way for a team to get a part.

Cheaper, plainly

We heavily undercut SendCutSend and goBILDA on the same parts. No warehouse, no payroll, no marketing budget — just three students, a CNC and material cost.

No shipping wait

We're in San Diego. Pick your parts up at the shop or get them delivered in 1–3 days. Fix a broken bracket before the next meeting, not after the next competition.

Built by FTC students

We're on FTC teams ourselves. You don't have to explain what a 8 mm grid or a REV rail is — we've designed around the same constraints all season.

Actually custom

Send a DXF, a STEP file, or honestly just a dimensioned sketch. One-off brackets are welcome — there's no minimum order and no setup fee on simple parts.

What we make

Our starting lineup. If it's flat, square, or round and made of aluminum, ask us about it.

Pocketed plates

Flat aluminum plates with an 8 mm hole grid and weight-saving pockets. Side plates, gearbox plates, mounting plates — cut to whatever outline you need.

  • Custom outlines and cutouts
  • Pocketed to cut weight
  • Clearance or tapped holes

Square beams

Threaded or unthreaded square beams in the lengths you actually use, drilled on the 8 mm grid so they mount up with the hardware you already own.

  • Threaded or unthreaded
  • Cut to length, no sawing
  • Deburred and ready to bolt

Standoffs

goBILDA-style aluminum standoffs in the common lengths, at a fraction of the price. Order five or order fifty — spacers are the last thing you should be rationing.

  • Common lengths in stock
  • Custom lengths on request
  • Bulk pricing that stays cheap

Custom brackets & one-offs

Broke a part mid-season? Need a bracket that nobody sells? Send us the geometry and we'll quote it — usually same day, usually in your hands that week.

Pricing & how to order

No account, no quote portal. Message us and you'll get a real number from a real person.

  1. 1

    Send us your part

    DXF, STEP, or a sketch with dimensions. Tell us the material thickness and how many you need.

  2. 2

    We quote it

    Usually within a few hours. We'll tell you up front if a feature is going to cost you, and what to change to make it cheaper.

  3. 3

    We machine it

    Most orders run in 1–3 days. We'll message you the moment they're off the machine.

  4. 4

    Pick up or get it delivered

    Grab them at the shop, or we deliver locally around San Diego. Outside the area? We can ship — you just lose the speed advantage.
