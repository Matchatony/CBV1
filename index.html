Why us

Our team competes in FTC, so hole-grid tolerances, weight targets, and competition deadlines aren't abstract to us. Late parts have cost us matches too, and our process is built around not repeating that.

Every member here is an active competitor. San Diego orders are typically hand-delivered rather than shipped. For anything outside standard plates or printed components, describe the application and we'll determine a fabrication approach.

About us

Crackbotics is operated by three students. Two are members of Runtime Terror in San Diego, and one of All Systems Go in Kentucky. After facing high costs for basic bracket fabrication — and already having the CAD experience and machine access — we began producing parts for other teams.

Before you submit

The key terms of every order, in one place. Full detail in the terms & conditions.

  • Estimates, confirmed by file — the price you see here is final once we see the actual design; if the real file costs more (extra material, tighter tolerances, extra setup), we tell you before starting, not after.
  • Payment — Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal, arranged by email once a quote is confirmed. We don't take payment on this site.
  • Pickup or local delivery — free pickup in San Diego, or delivery billed at cost (round-trip miles × $0.50/mile + round-trip drive time × $12–15/hr). We don't ship outside the local area.
  • Turnaround — 1–3 days is typical, not guaranteed; material availability or order volume can push it out, and we'll say so.
  • Cancellations — full refund any time before we start cutting. Once material is cut it isn't refundable, except when we made the mistake.
  • Deburring — included standard, no added charge.
