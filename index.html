Pricing

You pay for material and machine time. We don't have a warehouse, employees or ads to pay for, which is why our prices are lower than the big suppliers.

Send us your part and we'll give you a price the same day. If something in the design is expensive to cut, we'll tell you what to change.

  • No setup fee on simple parts
  • No minimum order, one bracket is fine
  • No rush fee, 1–3 days is the normal speed

Why us

We're on FTC teams too, so you don't have to explain what the grid is, why a part needs to be light, or why you need it before your next meet. We've had parts show up late and had to build around it ourselves.

Everyone here still competes. If you're in San Diego we'll usually just drop the part off instead of shipping it, and if what you need doesn't fit neatly into plates or printed parts, tell us what you're building and we'll figure it out.

About us

We're three students. Two of us are on Runtime Terror in San Diego, and Cade is on All Systems Go in Kentucky. We got tired of paying way too much for simple brackets. We already had the CAD skills and the machine time, so we started cutting parts for other teams.
